Geometric wooden mirror

A geometric mirror combining wood and reflective material.

Woodworkinggeometric designmirror

The idea

A mirror framed or structured with geometric wooden elements. The aspiration is a useful object with a strong relationship between geometry, wood and reflection, while exploring hands-on techniques. The confirmed facts are intentionally modest: I made the geometric wooden mirror. A convincing build story should be led by the actual shape and details, not a guessed construction process.

How it works

This is a physical build, not software to launch locally. Its use is the object’s actual purpose described above.
