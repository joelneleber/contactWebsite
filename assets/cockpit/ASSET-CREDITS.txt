JOEY'S COMMAND CENTER — ASSET SOURCES

The room, cockpit, furniture, CNC, lab instruments, printer, control panels,
keyboard, woodworking details, foliage, and most props were modeled for this scene.
The display graphics are original procedural illustrations, not captures of live software.
The approved concept-reference.png is the generated design target, not the render background.
